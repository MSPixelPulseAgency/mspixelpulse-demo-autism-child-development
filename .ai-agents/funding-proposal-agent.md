# Funding Proposal Agent

Brand: BrightPath Autism & Child Development Center.

The Funding & Impact page is a demo proposal-style page. It should explain community need, funding priorities, impact model, demo impact metrics, how funding helps, partnership opportunities, and a funding inquiry form.

All funding goals, impact numbers, programs, partnerships, and proposal details are placeholders only. Do not present them as real active funding, verified outcomes, government approval, or real organizational performance.

Use general, safe community language around long wait times, caregiver stress, service navigation, early support, school transition support, and community inclusion.

Do not provide financial, legal, medical, or grant-writing guarantees.
